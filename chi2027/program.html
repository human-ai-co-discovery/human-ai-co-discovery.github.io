Program

About the Workshop

This workshop explores human-AI co-discovery: how people and AI develop new questions, connections, and explanations together. A plausible or useful finding does not necessarily deepen people's understanding. An insight may also be new to a person or team without being new to a field.

We bring together researchers and practitioners from HCI, AI, and related fields to compare experiences of discovery with AI, including unsuccessful investigations. Through the four workshop questions, we will identify shared challenges, ethical considerations, and research priorities for supporting both new findings and human understanding. Our aim is to build a community and a shared research agenda for human-AI co-discovery.

For access requests and participation support, see our accessibility arrangements.

Schedule

We plan two consecutive 90-minute in-person sessions. The program is tentative and subject to workshop acceptance, speaker availability, and the conference schedule.

Session 1: Perspectives 90 min

  1. Welcome 5 min
  2. Keynote with Q&A 30 min
  3. Lightning talks: first block 25 min
  4. Human-AI panel discussion 30 min

Conference coffee break and poster exchange

Session 2: Co-Discovery 90 min

  1. Lightning talks: second block 20 min
  2. Interactive roundtable activity 50 min
  3. Group reports and collective discussion 15 min
  4. Closing and next steps 5 min

Session 1: Perspectives

  1. 5 min

    Welcome

    An overview of the workshop's goals and activities.

  2. 30 min

    Keynote with Q&A

    A keynote by Daniel McDuff (Google Research), followed by audience questions.

  3. 25 min

    Lightning talks: first block

    Authors of accepted contributions give brief talks of around three minutes each, split across two blocks with timings adjusted to the final program.

  4. 30 min

    Human-AI panel discussion

    Daniel McDuff joins Sherry Tongshuang Wu, Toby Jia-Jun Li, and a planned AI panelist for a moderated discussion with audience questions. The AI agent will respond to the same questions as the human panelists and to attendees' follow-up questions. Participants can challenge its assumptions, examine the evidence behind its claims, and compare its reasoning with the human panelists' perspectives.

Conference coffee break and poster exchange

We encourage authors to display posters on their own devices during the conference coffee break.

Session 2: Co-Discovery

  1. 20 min

    Lightning talks: second block

    The second block continues the talks by accepted contributors, with around three minutes per talk.

  2. 50 min

    Interactive roundtable activity

    With organizers facilitating, participants form seven cross-disciplinary groups to explore a question with AI using public or participant-approved materials. Each group identifies a turning point or unresolved difficulty and reflects on how its questions, explanations, or understanding developed, guided by the four workshop questions. Participants choose and approve any excerpts shared beyond their group.

  3. 15 min

    Group reports and collective discussion

    Each group gives a report of up to two minutes, sharing an exchange, insight, or unresolved difficulty and an open question. A collective discussion compares recurring themes and differences.

  4. 5 min

    Closing and next steps

    Organizers summarize the discussion and outline opportunities for continued exchange and collaboration after the workshop.

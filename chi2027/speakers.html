About the speaker

Daniel builds machine learning systems to sense, model, and synthesize human behavior, with a focus on health, physiology, and affective computing. His research spans camera-based physiological measurement, wearable foundation models, and health agents that draw personal and clinical insights from longitudinal sensor data.

Human-AI Panel

Daniel McDuff will also join the panel with Sherry Tongshuang Wu, Toby Jia-Jun Li, and an AI agent.

Sherry Tongshuang Wu

Carnegie Mellon University

Panelist

Sherry is an Assistant Professor in the Human-Computer Interaction Institute at Carnegie Mellon University. Her research connects HCI and natural language processing to help people work with imperfect AI through interactive debugging and correction. Her work has been published at ACL and CHI, and in TOCHI and TVCG.

Toby Jia-Jun Li

University of Notre Dame

Panelist

Toby is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Notre Dame. He leads the SaNDwich Lab and directs the Human-Centered Responsible AI Lab. He designs and studies interactive systems that help people create, configure, and extend AI-powered systems, including human-AI collaboration for the future of work.

An AI agent

Planned panelist

An AI agent is planned as the fourth panelist. It will respond to the same questions as the human speakers and to follow-up questions from attendees. Participants can challenge its assumptions, examine the evidence behind its claims, and compare its reasoning with the human panelists' perspectives.

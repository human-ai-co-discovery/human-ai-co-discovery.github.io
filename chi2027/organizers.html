Organizers

Meet the team and explore their research interests and selected work.

Zhihan Jiang

Columbia University

Personal website ↗

  • Human-AI co-sensemaking
  • Visual analytics

Her research focuses on human-AI co-sensemaking, examining how people interpret complex data and develop their own understanding through interaction with AI. She has built visual analytics tools for exploring children's health through multimodal data and a knowledge-empowered AI assistant for dietary monitoring. She has also proposed student-AI collaborative inquiry for learning.

Selected research
  • HealthPrism: A Visual Analytics System for Exploring Children's Physical and Mental Health Profiles with Multimodal Data IEEE TVCG · 2024
  • DietGlance: Dietary Monitoring and Personalized Analysis at a Glance with Knowledge-Empowered AI Assistant ACM Transactions on Computing for Healthcare · 2026

Her research connects data visualization, human-AI collaboration, and AI agents for scientific discovery and decision-making.

Whose A eureka is the moment something new becomes clear. When people and AI make discoveries together, we ask whose moment it is: new to whom, whose understanding changed, and who steered the inquiry and gets the credit.

Human-AI Co-Discovery of Knowledge

An illustrative human-AI co-discovery process

Panel 1, Formulate Questions: an interview excerpt reads, "I get so many notifications that it starts to feel overwhelming." The AI asks whether more notifications actually help people get things done faster. Panel 2, Explore Unexpected Connections: the researcher says the logs suggest they do, but experienced users also seem to get more notifications. The AI notes that within each experience group, more notifications are linked to slower work. Two interview excerpts read, "They keep me on track" and "They stress me out." Panel 3, Construct, Challenge, and Revise Explanations: the researcher suggests that experience may be masking the cost of interruptions and asks whether the pattern holds across different kinds of tasks. The AI says it seems strongest during focused work and proposes a controlled study. A new question asks how task and context shape people's experiences of notifications. Arrows lead from panel 1 to 2, from 2 down to 3, and from 3 back up to 1.

AI systems increasingly help formulate ideas, explore data, and develop explanations. Yet a plausible or useful finding does not necessarily expand or deepen human knowledge. Discovery and understanding can reinforce one another: an unexpected connection may change how people frame a problem and what they ask next.

This workshop explores human-AI co-discovery of knowledge: how people and AI develop new questions, connections, and explanations together. We invite researchers and practitioners to examine human agency, evidence, learning, and the recognition of contributions, and to develop a shared research agenda for discovery with AI.

We welcome position papers, empirical studies, system descriptions, design concepts, critical perspectives, and work in progress on human-AI co-discovery.

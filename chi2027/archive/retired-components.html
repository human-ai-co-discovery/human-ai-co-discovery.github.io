1. Home hero

Proposed workshop @ CHI 2027 Pittsburgh, PA, USA

Whose

A eureka is the moment something new becomes clear. When people and AI make discoveries together, we ask whose moment it is: new to whom, whose understanding changed, and who steered the inquiry and gets the credit.

Human-AI Co-Discovery of Knowledge

2. Question cards

  • Q1Co-discovery process How do people and AI develop questions, connections, and explanations together?

    People and AI can build on, challenge, or redirect one another's suggestions as they explore evidence and develop provisional conclusions. How can human-AI interaction help people recognize when an unexpected connection is worth pursuing, rather than simply accepting an AI suggestion?

  • Q2Agency & credit How do people guide co-discovery, and how are contributions recognized?

    As people delegate work to AI, they decide when to intervene, revise a plan, or decline assistance, and records of decisions and revisions can make human and AI contributions visible. How should credit, intellectual ownership, and responsibility be understood and negotiated when discoveries emerge from human-AI collaboration?

  • Q3Human understanding What do people come to understand through co-discovery?

    Human-AI co-discovery may change how people frame a problem, what they notice, and what they ask next, even when an investigation remains inconclusive. How can reflection, learning, and changes in understanding be recognized alongside the findings that people and AI produce?

  • Q4What counts as discovery What counts as a discovery, for whom, and on what evidence?

    Is an insight new to an individual, a team, or a field, and do its claims hold up against counter-evidence or alternative explanations? Concerns about scientific monocultures also raise the question of whether co-discovery can broaden, rather than narrow, the questions a community pursues.

4. Topic cards

Q1How do people and AI develop questions, connections, and explanations together?

  • Interpreting together

    How people and AI make sense of data and existing knowledge, formulate questions, and revise interpretations as evidence develops.

  • Serendipity and unexpected connections

    How interaction with AI shapes the recognition of unexpected patterns, relationships, and possibilities, and how people decide which connections are worth pursuing.

  • Developing and testing explanations

    How people and AI construct, compare, challenge, and revise explanations, models, and representations, including the assumptions that underpin them.

Q2How do people guide co-discovery, and how are contributions recognized?

  • Human agency in co-discovery

    How people direct inquiry, work with proactive AI agents, delegate tasks, and decide when to intervene, redirect an exchange, or pursue another direction.

  • Whose contribution, whose discovery?

    How human and AI contributions become visible, and how credit, intellectual ownership, and responsibility are understood and negotiated.

Q3What do people come to understand through co-discovery?

  • Human understanding and learning

    What people come to understand through co-discovery, how that understanding changes over time, and how reflection and further questioning develop even when an investigation remains inconclusive.

Q4What counts as a discovery, for whom, and on what evidence?

  • Evidence, uncertainty, and failure

    How people and AI seek counter-evidence, recognize misleading patterns, work with uncertainty, and learn from unsuccessful inquiry.

  • What counts as a discovery?

    What makes a connection or finding new and worthwhile for an individual, a team, or a field, and how novelty and value vary across research and practice.

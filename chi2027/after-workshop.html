Follow-up

These plans are subject to acceptance of the workshop proposal.

Sharing Materials

With authors' permission, accepted papers and workshop materials will remain available on this website, including an index of papers that authors choose to post on arXiv. We do not plan separate archival proceedings.

A Joint Perspective

Interested participants may also contribute to a follow-up perspective or research-agenda paper for an HCI venue, with authorship reflecting substantive contributions to synthesis and writing.

Continuing the Conversation

You can already ask public questions and reply on GitHub through our community question wall. An opt-in Discord community is coming soon for discussion, resource sharing, and connections before, during, and after the workshop. It will be open to interested researchers and practitioners, whether or not they attend.

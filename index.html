01About

Discovery with AI, and the people who understand it

AI systems increasingly take part in the activities through which new knowledge is developed: formulating questions, surfacing unexpected connections, and proposing explanations. A useful finding, however, does not necessarily deepen people's understanding of its evidence or implications.

Human-AI Co-Discovery brings together researchers and practitioners in human-AI interaction, visual analytics, AI for science, design, and learning to study how people and AI shape inquiry together, how people keep agency over it, how contributions are recognized, and what counts as a discovery, and for whom, across scientific research and everyday knowledge work.
